HOW TO READ THIS

Same method for every pitcher: a computer tracked only the pitcher's body from two camera angles and tried to name the pitch type before release, on games it had never seen. Pts is the edge over always guessing his most common pitch. Clear means a hitter who knows what to look for could use it; weak is a small but real edge; no tell means his body gave nothing away.

Windup with the bases empty, stretch with runners on. A verdict needs at least 150 pitches of each type in that delivery, on tracked video; the center-field camera in some parks sits too high and far to follow the legs, and those games count only on the hitter view. "Pitches with a cue" is the share of pitches in processed games where the video read the type with confidence at a moment that carries a real edge; it only counts where a tell was found.
